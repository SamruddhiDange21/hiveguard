📖 CONDITION INTERPRETATION LAYER

HEALTHY

Stable temperature (33.5–35.5°C), stable humidity (50–65%), gradual weight progression.

COLONY STRESS

Temperature deviation, persistent elevated humidity, progressive weight decline.

SWARMING RISK

Rapid biomass loss over short periods, ventilation and thermal pattern changes.

VARROA RISK

Possible Varroa-related risk pattern: persistent microclimate depression and weight decline. (Not confirmed infection).

SENSOR ANOMALY

Abrupt impossible value in single sensor (e.g. temp >41°C) with others stable.

⚠️

Research Scope, Limitations & Veterinary Disclaimers

  • Decision-Support, Not Autonomous Diagnosis: The predictive models and anomaly scores provide decision support for apiary managers. They highlight statistical microclimate deviations and are not definitive veterinary diagnoses for parasitic diseases.
  • Continuous Hive Weight Availability: While the legacy synthetic prototype modeled continuous 15-minute gross weight, the real MSPB research dataset recorded hive weight periodically at milestone inspections (pre-winter, post-winter, and honey yield). The framework handles missing channels transparently without fabricating substitute values.
  • Non-Invasive Verification: High anomaly scores trigger recommendations for targeted physical checks (e.g. bottom sticky-board washes, inspection of brood comb cappings) rather than automated medication.

🛡️ Strict Data-Leakage Prevention Protocol

In accordance with rigorous time-series machine-learning principles, HIVEGUARD applies the following safeguards:

1. Chronological Splitting

Train and evaluation sets are split chronologically within each hive. Future measurements are never shuffled into training folds.

2. Zero Target Leakage

Target columns (condition_label, anomaly) are strictly isolated and never passed into the feature matrix.

3. Backward-Looking Features

All rolling statistics, medians, and MAD values use backward-looking historical windows with zero future lookahead.

ABOUT HIVEGUARD

          
Precision Apiculture IoT & Anomaly Prediction System

        

        

          
HIVEGUARD is an autonomous apiary monitoring system designed to protect Apis cerana indica colonies through continuous sensor telemetry and machine learning.

          
Target Anomaly Categories:

          
    
            
  • Healthy: Optimal autonomous brood nest thermoregulation (~34.4°C).
    • 
            
  • Colony Stress: Synchronous thermal and humidity deviations with biomass decline.
    • 
            
  • Swarming Risk: Sudden biomass reduction signaling colony division.
    • 
            
  • Varroa Risk: Chronic microclimate depression requiring physical sticky-board washes.
    • 
            
  • Sensor Anomaly: Isolated hardware probe errors.
    • 
          

          
Technical Stack:

          
Backend: Flask (Python 3.10+), scikit-learn, XGBoost, pandas, NumPy.

          Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, Chart.js.

          Communication: REST API JSON endpoints.
